A per-object snapshot of the live `public` schema's permissions
posture at the migration set checked into this repo. Sibling to
[`shared/db/types.ts`](./types.ts) (row shapes); the two together
are the in-repo answer to "what is true for `public.X` today."

## Roles in scope

Per-role coverage spans `anon`, `authenticated`, `service_role`,
and the `PUBLIC` pseudo-role. Privileges granted to `PUBLIC`
reach every role (including `anon` and `authenticated`). The
`postgres` owner role is implicit — owners hold every privilege
on objects they own.

## Privileges in scope

Tables and views: the SQL-standard grantable set — `SELECT`,
`INSERT`, `UPDATE`, `DELETE`, `TRUNCATE`, `REFERENCES`, `TRIGGER`.
Functions: `EXECUTE`.
